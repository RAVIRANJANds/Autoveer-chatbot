STEP 1: Blank Google Sheet banayein.

STEP 2: Service account JSON (credentials.json) ki email ko Sheet par Editor access dein.

STEP 3: backend\.env
  GOOGLE_SHEET_ID=spreadsheet_id_from_url
  GEMINI_API_KEY=your_key

STEP 4: Neeche 6 worksheets (tabs) banayein — NAMES EXACT (underscore wale).

STEP 5: Har tab ki ROW 1 mein ye headers copy-paste karein (ek row, columns left to right).
